# mFRR Profit Tracker

Track Kratt mFRR/aFRR activations dispatched through Qilowatt, the energy your battery delivered against the baseline, market prices and the resulting profit, in near real time from Home Assistant sensors.

The UI opens from the Home Assistant sidebar. Summary sensors (`sensor.mfrr_net_today`, `sensor.mfrr_net_total`, …) are published back to Home Assistant for dashboards and automations.
